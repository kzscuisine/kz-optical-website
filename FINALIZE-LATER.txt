KZ Optical customer-ready framework
==================================
This build intentionally centralizes unfinished business values in:
  lib/store-config.ts

Final setup later can update in one pass:
- frame price(s)
- lens prices and upgrades
- shipping/free-shipping thresholds
- contact email
- inventory mode
- final prescription workflow

External/account-dependent launch steps remain separate:
- payment provider credentials
- transactional email credentials
- domain/DNS
- final legal/compliance wording and policy approval
- actual product-specific stock/colour/size data and product imagery where needed
